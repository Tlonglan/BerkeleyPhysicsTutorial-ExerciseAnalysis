# 矢量

## 位置矢量

### 1 题
#### 1.1
因为是东北方向，即矢量 $\bm{r}$ 与x轴正方向、y轴正方向的夹角均为45$\degree$，所以矢量 $\bm{r}$ 在x轴、y轴上的投影大小是一样的。即 $r_x = r_y$ ，所以有
$$
\begin{aligned}
    &{r_x}^2 + {r_y}^2 = 2 {r_x}^2 = 10^2 \\
    &x = 5\sqrt{2} = y
\end{aligned}
$$

因此
$$
\begin{aligned}
    & \bm{r} = 5 \sqrt{2} \hat{\bm{x}} + 5 \sqrt{2} \hat{\bm{y}} + 2 \hat{\bm{z}} \\
    & \lvert{\bm{r}}\rvert = \sqrt{\sqrt{50}^2 + \sqrt{50}^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{104}
\end{aligned}
$$


设 $\bm{r}$ 的方向为 $\hat{\bm{r}} = A\hat{\bm{x}} + B\hat{\bm{y}} + C\hat{\bm{z}}$ ，则有

$$
\left\{
    \begin{aligned}
        &\frac{B}{A} = 1 \\
        &\frac{C}{A} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{5} \\
        &A^2+B^2+C^2 = 1^2
    \end{aligned}
\right.
\Rightarrow \quad
\left\{
    \begin{aligned}
        &A = \frac{5}{\sqrt{52}} \\
        &B = \frac{5}{\sqrt{52}} \\
        &C = \sqrt{\frac{2}{52}}
    \end{aligned}
\right.
$$


#### 1.2
因为是东南方向，即矢量 $\bm{r}$ 与x轴正方向、y轴负方向的夹角均为45$\degree$，所以矢量 $\bm{r}$在x轴、y轴上的投影大小相等，方向相反。即$r_x = -r_y$，所以有

$$
\begin{aligned}
    &{r_x}^2 + {r_y}^2 = 2 {r_x}^2 = 5^2 \\
    &x = \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2} \\
    &y = -\frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

因此
$$
\begin{aligned}
    &\bm{r} =\frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2} \hat{\bm{x}} -\frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2} \hat{\bm{y}} - 5 \hat{\bm{z}} \\
    &\lvert{\bm{r}}\rvert = \sqrt{{\frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}}^2 +{\frac{5\sqrt{2}}{2}}^2 + {-5}^2} = 5\sqrt{2} 
\end{aligned}
$$

设$\bm{r} $的方向为 $ \hat{\bm{r}} = A\hat{\bm{x}} + B\hat{\bm{y}} + C\hat{\bm{z}} $，则有

$$
\left\{
    \begin{aligned}
        &\frac{B}{A} = -1 \\
        &\frac{C}{A} =-\sqrt{2} \\
        &A^2+B^2+C^2 = 1^2
    \end{aligned}
\right.
\Rightarrow \quad
\left\{
    \begin{aligned}
        &A = \frac{1}{2} \\
        &B = -\frac{1}{2} \\
        &C = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
    \end{aligned}
\right.
$$



#### 1.3

因为是西北方向，即矢量 $\bm{r}$ 与x轴负方向、y轴正方向的夹角均为45$\degree$，所以矢量$\bm{r}$在x轴、y轴上的投影大小相等，方向相反。即$-r_x = r_y$, 所以有
$$
\begin{aligned}
    &{r_x}^2 + {r_y}^2 = 2 {r_y}^2 = 1^2 \\
    &x = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}  \\
    &y = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$


因此
$$
\begin{aligned}
    &\bm{r} =-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \hat{\bm{x}} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \hat{\bm{y}} + 6 \hat{\bm{z}} \\
    &\lvert{\bm{r}}\rvert = \sqrt{{-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}^2 +{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{37}
\end{aligned}
$$

设$\bm{r} $的方向为 $ \hat{\bm{r}} = A\hat{\bm{x}} + B\hat{\bm{y}} + C\hat{\bm{z}} $ , 则有

$$
\left\{
    \begin{aligned}
    &\frac{B}{A} = -1 \\
    &\frac{C}{A} =-6 \sqrt{2} \\
    &A^2+B^2+C^2 = 1^2
    \end{aligned}
\right.
\Rightarrow \quad
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
    &A = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{74}} \\
    &B = \frac{1}{\sqrt{74}} \\
    &C = 12\sqrt{37}
    \end{aligned}
\right.
$$
